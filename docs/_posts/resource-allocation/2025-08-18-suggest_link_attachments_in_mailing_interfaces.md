t-description: "Instead of attaching large files directly to emails, email clients can suggest or automate the use of cloud storage links. This avoids redundant file storage, reduces energy consumption in data centers, and often improves download performance for recipients."
t-participant: "End-users (email senders)"
t-artifact: "Email clients and mailing interfaces"
t-context: "Daily professional or personal communication using email"
t-feature: "File attachment handling"
t-intent: "To reduce redundant file storage and improve energy efficiency when sending large files"
t-targetQA: "Resource efficiency"
t-relatedQA: "Performance, usability"
t-measuredimpact: "Email attachments can create ~55,000 duplicate files per user per year; link-based sharing reduces this to ~5,000. While the study did not quantify energy directly, fewer duplicates mean less storage demand and thus lower data center energy use."
t-source: "Maliha Nawshin Rahman, Maël Madon, Patricia Lago (2024) 'Sufficient Use of the Cloud for Work: Practitioners’ Perception and Potential for Energy Saving'"
